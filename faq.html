GOOD QUESTIONS

FAQ

ask us anything (within reason)
What time should I arrive?
By 5:00 PM. The ceremony starts at 5:30 PM sharp — and when we say sharp, we mean it. Don’t be the person walking down the aisle before Shirley does.
What’s the dress code?
Cocktail attire. Fresh colors, linens, and florals fit right in — it’s a Tuscan garden party, after all.
Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?
Outdoors! Villa Toscana has beautiful gardens we'll be spending the evening in. January in Florida is usually perfect.
Can I bring a plus-one?
Check your invitation — if your invite says “and guest,” bring them! If you’re not sure, just ask us.
Is there parking at the venue?
Yes, there’s parking on site. Uber and Lyft work great too if you plan to make the most of the open bar.
How do I RSVP?
Hit the yellow RSVP button at the top of any page. Please respond by the date on your invitation so we can save you a seat (and a plate).
