GOOD QUESTIONS

FAQ

ask us anything (within reason)
Where exactly is the wedding?
Villa Toscana — 27550 SW 222nd Ave, Homestead, FL 33031. Tap the address and your maps app will take you straight there. It’s about 40 minutes south of Miami International.
What time should I arrive?
By 5:00 PM. The ceremony starts at 5:30 PM sharp — and when we say sharp, we mean it. Don’t be the person walking down the aisle before Shirley does.
What’s the dress code?
Anywhere from cocktail to formal — if it makes you feel wonderful, it’s right. Already have a formal gown or a suit you love? Wear it. For inspiration, think Tuscan garden: florals, linens, and pretty blues, greens, and yellows. The only rule is comfortable enough to dance in.
Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?
Outdoors! Villa Toscana has beautiful gardens we’ll be spending the evening in. January in Florida is usually perfect — pleasant evenings in the 60s–70s.
Who is included on my invitation?
Your Paperless Post invitation lists everyone in your party by name, and we’re only able to host the guests named there — we promise it’s nothing personal, just a garden with a guest limit. One sweet exception: babies under 2 are always welcome to join you.
Are kids invited?
Little ones under 2 are welcome, no questions asked. Older kids join us when they’re named on your invitation — if yours are listed, we can’t wait to see them on the dance floor.
Is there parking at the venue?
Yes, there’s parking on site — with valet to help you park when you arrive. Uber and Lyft might work on the way there, but make arrangements for the way back; it’s in Homestead, after all.
Is there a shuttle?
Yes! We’re running a complimentary round-trip shuttle between Hollywood Beach and the venue. Pickup spot and times are being finalized — check the Travel & Stay page for updates.
How do I RSVP?
Your invitation arrives by email from Paperless Post, and that’s where you RSVP — for every guest on your invite. Please respond by the date on your invitation so we can save you a seat (and a plate). Details on the RSVP page.
What if I have dietary restrictions?
The Paperless Post RSVP asks for meal and dietary preferences for each guest — fill it in there and we’ll make sure everyone gets a plate they’ll love.
